Wir setzen keine Cookies. Wenn Sie zwischen Deutsch und Englisch wechseln, speichert Ihr Browser diese Auswahl lokal auf Ihrem Gerät (Local Storage, Eintrag „athidhi_lang“). We do not set cookies. When you switch between German and English, your browser stores the choice locally on your device (local storage, entry "athidhi_lang"). You can delete the entry in your browser at any time.

5. Fonts

The fonts "Cormorant Garamond" and "Proza Libre" are hosted locally on our server. No data is sent to Google or any other font provider when you open a page.

If you use the contact form or the enquiry forms, we process what you enter (for example name, email address, phone number, preferred date, number of guests and your message) to answer your enquiry. Data is only transmitted when you submit the form.

  • The contact form sends your message by email to info@athidhi.de through the service Web3Forms (api.web3forms.com), which acts as our service provider for delivery. [Please check the data processing agreement and any transfer to third countries]

You can apply through the Careers page. We process your name, contact details, the role you want, details of your experience and current employer, and the CV you upload. The data is stored with Supabase (see section 6). The legal basis is Section 26 BDSG and Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. We delete application data no later than [period, e.g. 6 months] after the process ends, unless you agreed to longer storage.
